For twenty years, the job was simple to describe: rank on Google. You earned links, matched intent, and climbed the blue links. That game still matters — but it is no longer the whole board.

A growing share of searches never produce a click. Someone asks a question, an AI engine answers it directly, and the user moves on. Google's AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini — they read the web, synthesise an answer, and cite a handful of sources. The new question isn't "can I rank?" It's "will I be the source the answer is built from?"

That discipline has a name: Answer Engine Optimisation.

## SEO and AEO are cousins, not rivals

Most of what makes you rank also makes you citable: genuine expertise, clear structure, fast pages, trustworthy signals. AEO doesn't replace SEO — it extends it. The shift is in emphasis.

- **From keywords to questions.** Answer engines work in natural language. Content organised around the real questions people ask gets pulled into answers more readily than content stuffed with keywords.
- **From pages to passages.** Engines lift specific passages, not whole pages. Self-contained, well-labelled sections win.
- **From traffic to citation.** Success increasingly looks like being named as a source, even when the click never comes. Brand recall compounds quietly.

## A framework you can start this quarter

You don't need to rebuild your site. You need to make it legible to machines that read for meaning.

1. **Answer the question in the first two sentences.** Lead with the conclusion, then support it. Engines reward content that resolves intent quickly.
2. **Add structured data.** Schema markup — Article, FAQ, Organisation, Person — tells engines what your content *is*. It is one of the highest-leverage, lowest-effort moves available.
3. **Build topical depth, not one-off posts.** A cluster of related, interlinked pieces signals authority on a subject far more than a scattered blog.
4. **Make expertise explicit.** Named authors, credentials, and consistent identity across the web build the entity signals engines use to decide who to trust.
5. **Keep it fast and clean.** The technical fundamentals that helped SEO help AEO too. Nothing here asks you to abandon what already works.

## The quiet advantage

Here's the part most teams miss: AEO compounds. A page engineered to be the clearest answer to a real question keeps earning citations long after you publish it. That's the same logic that made content marketing powerful in the first place — only now the distribution layer includes every AI assistant your buyer talks to.

The search box is becoming a chat box. The brands that adapt early won't just be found. They'll be the answer.
